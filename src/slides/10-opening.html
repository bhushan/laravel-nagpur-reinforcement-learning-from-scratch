Reinforcement learning, from a failing test

You already train things with red and green. Here is how a machine does it.

Bhushan Gaikwad 45 min Laravel Nagpur · 27 Nov 2026

0:30. One idea for the whole talk: you already work in a loop where a machine tells you red or green. Reinforcement learning is that loop with the human taken out.

Point at the dots while they fill. Each row is one attempt at the same task. Each dot is one hidden test. Mostly red at first, then green. That drift is what we will spend 45 minutes understanding.

Say it is illustrative: this is the shape of learning, not a recorded run.
